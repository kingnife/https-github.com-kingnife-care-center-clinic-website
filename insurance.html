Your Right to a Good Faith Estimate (GFE)

Federal No Surprises Act Disclosure (Public Law 116-260)

Under Section 2799B-6 of the Public Health Service Act, healthcare providers and facilities are required to inform individuals who are not enrolled in an insurance plan or coverage, or a Federal healthcare program, or who are not seeking to file a claim with their plan or coverage, both orally and in writing of their ability, upon request or at the time of scheduling healthcare items and services, to receive a "Good Faith Estimate" of expected charges.

  • You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate for the total expected cost of any non-emergency items or services, including related medical tests, prescription reviews, and psychotherapy sessions.
  • Make sure your healthcare provider gives you a Good Faith Estimate in writing at least 1 business day before your medical service or item.
  • If you receive a bill that is at least $400 more than your Good Faith Estimate, you have the right to dispute the bill with the federal dispute resolution entity.
  • For questions or more information about your right to a Good Faith Estimate, visit www.cms.gov/nosurprises or call 1-800-985-3059.
