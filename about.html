Governance & Ethics

Clinical Integrity & Regulatory Transparency

HIPAA Privacy & Data Protection Pledge

Under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), your protected health information (PHI) is safeguarded with bank-grade 256-bit AES encryption across all digital communications, electronic health records (EHR), and telehealth video streams. We never sell, monetize, or disclose your clinical records to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

State Licensure: All practicing clinicians hold active, unencumbered licenses with the Medical Board of California or the California Board of Behavioral Sciences (BBS).
Emergency Scope: Care Center provides outpatient ambulatory care during standard operating hours. We are not an acute psychiatric emergency facility or hospital trauma unit.
Non-Discrimination: Care Center complies with applicable Federal civil rights laws and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability, or sex.
