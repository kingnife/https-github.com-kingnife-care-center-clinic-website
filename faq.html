Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers about our clinical tracks, insurance in-network benefits, Superbill reimbursements, and sanctuary appointments.

Traditional healthcare often limits appointments to rushed 12–15 minute windows, where clinicians are hurried through electronic checklists. At Care Center, every initial diagnostic intake is unhurried (60 to 90 minutes), followed by 30-to-50 minute follow-ups. Our physical rooms are sound-engineered to below 32 dB with HEPA H13 filtration and natural lighting to ensure your autonomic nervous system remains calm and receptive.
Unlike fragmented standalone offices, our clinicians share a single unified, HIPAA-encrypted medical chart. If you are receiving individual psychotherapy and also see our psychiatrist for medication management, your providers consult weekly to reconcile your treatment progress. Primary care physicians and psychiatrists cross-reference blood chemistry and pharmacogenomic data before prescribing.
We are directly in-network with Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross (California), Cigna Healthcare, and Medicare Part B. For in-network members, we submit all insurance claims directly; you are only responsible for your designated specialist copay, coinsurance, or remaining deductible.
If your plan is through another insurer (such as UnitedHealthcare, Oxford, or Humana PPO), you can pay using cash, credit card, HSA, or FSA, and we immediately provide an itemized CMS-1500 compliant "Superbill" with diagnostic ICD-10 and procedure CPT codes. Most out-of-network PPO plans reimburse between 60% and 80% of allowable clinical costs directly back to you.
Yes. All Care Center physicians, psychiatrists, and therapists hold active California statewide licenses. We provide 256-bit AES encrypted high-definition video rooms that connect directly in your browser without downloading proprietary third-party software. E-prescriptions are routed automatically to your preferred local California pharmacy.
GeneSight® is an objective genetic test performed with a quick, painless cheek swab in our sanctuary or via a home test kit. It analyzes your unique liver enzyme metabolizer genes (CYP450) and neurotransmitter receptor variations to identify which psychiatric medications are most likely to be effective and which carry high risks of side effects. It is covered by Medicare and many private insurers.
When you enter 450 Sutter Street Suite 1200, you don't have to wait in a crowded line or sign paperwork on a communal clipboard. Simply tap "Check In" on your smartphone or our lobby tablet, enter your name, and take a seat in our quiet botanical sensory lounge. Your doctor receives an immediate silent notification on their secure terminal.
We operate under strict HIPAA Compliance and HITECH security rules. All electronic medical records, patient communications, lab orders, and video sessions utilize 256-bit AES encryption. Psychotherapy process notes are maintained under elevated privacy protections and are never disclosed without your explicit written authorization.
Care Center is an outpatient ambulatory medical and psychiatric facility operating Monday through Saturday. We are not an emergency room or acute crisis stabilization unit. In the event of a life-threatening medical emergency, call 911 immediately. If you or a loved one is experiencing thoughts of suicide, call or text 988 to connect with the 24/7 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
